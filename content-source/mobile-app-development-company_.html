What is Mobile App Development?

Mobile app development is the process of creating software applications that run on smartphones and tablets, either natively (iOS or Android) or cross-platform using frameworks like React Native. A well-built mobile app provides users with fast, offline-capable, and secure access to your product — driving engagement, retention, and brand loyalty far more effectively than mobile websites alone.

Key Benefits

  • Direct push notification engagement
  • Offline data storage & sync
  • Biometric security & device features
  • Higher customer retention rates

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I build a native or cross-platform mobile app?

We recommend React Native for 90% of business applications. It shares a single codebase across iOS and Android, reducing development costs by 40–60% while delivering near-native performance. We recommend native (Swift/Kotlin) only when you need deep hardware access, such as AR features, custom camera processing, or complex device-level integrations.

How much does mobile app development cost in India?

A simple informational or catalogue app typically costs ₹3–8 Lakhs. A feature-rich consumer app with authentication, payments, push notifications, and a backend API ranges from ₹10–30 Lakhs. Complex platforms with real-time features, IoT integrations, or AI personalization can range from ₹30–80 Lakhs.

How long does mobile app development take?

A simple MVP app can be ready in 8–12 weeks. A full-featured consumer app with backend, admin panel, and third-party integrations typically takes 4–6 months. Complex platforms with real-time features, advanced analytics, and multi-role dashboards take 6–10 months.

Do you publish apps to the Apple App Store and Google Play Store?

Yes. We handle the full App Store and Google Play publishing process — including store listing optimisation, screenshots, privacy policy setup, metadata, and compliance review submissions. Apple's review typically takes 24–48 hours; Google Play takes 1–3 days for new apps.

Can AAHA Solutions build apps for UK and European markets?

Yes. We have built consumer and enterprise apps for clients in the UK and Germany — with GDPR-compliant data handling, localised UI/UX, multi-currency payment integrations (Stripe UK, PayPal), and Apple Pay / Google Pay support for European markets.

Do you offer post-launch support, bug fixes, and OS updates?

Yes. We offer monthly SLA maintenance contracts covering crash monitoring, OS compatibility updates (iOS / Android new versions), performance optimisations, security patches, and new feature releases — ensuring your app stays live, fast, and compliant with App Store policies.
