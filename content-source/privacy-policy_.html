  2. Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 2026

At AAHA Solutions ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal and business information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website (www.aahasolutions.com), communicate with us, or engage our software development, artificial intelligence, cloud consulting, and technology services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you directly provide to us or that is automatically generated during your interactions with our website:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, business email address, phone number, company name, and location when you submit an inquiry or schedule a consultation.
  • Project Requirements: Technical specifications, project scope details, documentation, and preferences shared for discovery sessions and proposals.
  • Automated Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, operating systems, and pages visited, collected via cookies and web analytics to improve website performance.
  • Communication Records: Transcripts of emails, chat messages, and consultation notes exchanged with our technical and sales teams.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding promptly to your service inquiries, RFPs, and consultation requests.
  • Preparing tailored software architectures, proposals, non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), and project contracts.
  • Delivering and supporting our contracted AI, SaaS, mobile, ERP, and cloud engineering services.
  • Maintaining website security, detecting unauthorized access, and optimizing user experience.
  • Sending project updates, technical insights, and relevant company news (you may opt out at any time).

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality (NDAs)

Intellectual Property & Non-Disclosure Guarantee

We sign comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) before discussing sensitive project logic. All proprietary code, algorithms, business assets, and client data remain 100% the sole property of our clients upon project milestone completion.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party advertisers. We may only share information with trusted infrastructure service providers (e.g., secure hosting providers, transactional email services such as PHPMailer/SMTP) solely for fulfilling contracted development activities and under strict confidentiality obligations.

5. International Compliance (GDPR & Global Standards)

As a technology partner serving clients across India, the United Kingdom, Europe, and globally, AAHA Solutions adheres to international data governance principles, including the European Union and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of Access & Portability: You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of your personal contact records from our active marketing systems.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses standard cookies and aggregated tracking to analyze traffic flow, optimize page load speed, and enhance navigation. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings without affecting core website readability.

7. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including SSL/TLS encryption, firewall protection, role-based database access, and regular vulnerability scanning to prevent unauthorized data access or breach.
