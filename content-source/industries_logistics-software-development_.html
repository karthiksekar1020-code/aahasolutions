Frequently Asked Questions

Can your software integrate with our existing vehicle GPS trackers and hardware?

Yes. We support standard telematics protocols (CAN-bus, J1939, OBD-II) and direct API integration with popular GPS tracking vendors (Teltonika, Geotab, Samsara, CalAmp), ingesting real-time coordinates, speed, fuel levels, and diagnostic trouble codes.

How do you handle proof of delivery in remote areas with poor network coverage?

Our mobile driver apps are designed offline-first using SQLite and local caching. Drivers can capture digital signatures, photos, and timestamps without an active network. All delivery records automatically sync to the central dispatch dashboard the moment cellular connection is regained.

Can the platform calculate dynamic freight rates and generate invoices automatically?

Yes. The system can calculate shipping charges based on weight, volume, route distance, fuel surcharges, and special handling fees. Once proof of delivery is confirmed, PDF invoices and Bill of Lading documents are generated and synced to your accounting ERP.
