Frequently Asked Questions

Why should my enterprise choose open source software over proprietary platforms?

Open source solutions provide full intellectual property control, zero recurring per-user licensing costs, transparent security verified by global developer communities, and the freedom to customize every aspect of the software to match your exact business logic.

How does AAHA Solutions ensure open-source code security and compliance?

We follow rigorous DevSecOps practices: continuous dependency vulnerability scanning, automated penetration tests, adhering to OWASP top 10 benchmarks, strict licensing compliance checks, and regular security patch updates.

Can you migrate our legacy application to a modern open-source framework?

Yes. We regularly migrate legacy monolithic architectures, outdated PHP/ASP apps, and proprietary databases to modern open source stacks such as Laravel, Django, Node.js, and PostgreSQL/MySQL with minimal downtime.
