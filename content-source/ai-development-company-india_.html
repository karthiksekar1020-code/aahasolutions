Frequently Asked Questions

What does an AI development company do?

An AI development company designs, trains, and deploys intelligent software systems that automate decisions and extract insights from data. AAHA Solutions builds custom NLP chatbots, predictive ML models, computer vision systems, and Generative AI integrations for startups and enterprises across India and the UK.

How much does AI development cost in India?

AI development costs vary by complexity. An API-based chatbot integration typically starts from ₹2–5 Lakhs. A fully custom-trained machine learning model with data pipelines and deployment infrastructure ranges from ₹10–40 Lakhs depending on dataset size and desired accuracy targets.

How long does it take to build an AI solution?

A prototype or GPT-powered chatbot integration can go live in 4–6 weeks. Custom-trained predictive models with labelled datasets, validation pipelines, and cloud deployment typically take 3–6 months. Enterprise AI platforms with multiple integrated models can take 6–12 months.

Can AAHA Solutions build custom AI chatbots?

Yes. We build context-aware AI chatbots using OpenAI GPT APIs, LLaMA, and Dialogflow — trained on your own knowledge base, product catalogue, or support documentation. Our chatbots integrate with WhatsApp, websites, and CRM systems to handle customer queries 24/7.

Do you provide AI consulting for UK and European clients?

Yes. AAHA Solutions has delivered AI projects for clients in the UK and Europe. We operate across time zones with dedicated project managers and provide weekly sprint reports, making remote collaboration seamless for international clients.

What AI technologies and frameworks do you specialise in?

Our AI team works with TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, OpenAI GPT-4, LangChain, Hugging Face Transformers, OpenCV, and FAISS for vector search. We deploy on AWS SageMaker, Google Vertex AI, and Azure Machine Learning.
