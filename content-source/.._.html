Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our engagement models, timelines, and development lifecycle.

What engagement models do you offer?

We offer three flexible models:
  • Dedicated Development Team: Full-time developers managed directly by you or our project manager.
  • Time & Material (T&M): Payment based on actual hours spent, perfect for evolving requirements.
  • Fixed Price: A pre-defined cost and scope outline for structured projects.

How long does a typical software project take?

Timelines depend heavily on the project size:
  • Small / MVP projects: 6 to 10 weeks.
  • Medium sized applications: 3 to 6 months.
  • Enterprise software solutions: 6 months or more, usually built in phased Agile sprints.

How do you ensure code security and IP protection?

We prioritize data governance and IP security. We sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) before discussion, use secure code repositories with role-based access, run automated vulnerability checks, and guarantee that 100% of the Intellectual Property (IP) belongs to you upon project sign-off.
