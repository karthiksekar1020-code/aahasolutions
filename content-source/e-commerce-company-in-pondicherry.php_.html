Frequently Asked Questions

Which e-commerce platform is best for my business?

The ideal platform depends on your business model and catalog volume: Magento/Adobe Commerce is recommended for large enterprise B2B/B2C stores; WooCommerce for content-driven boutiques; and Custom Headless or Laravel setups for high-speed bespoke ordering pipelines.

How do you handle payment security and PCI-DSS compliance?

We implement tokenized payment gateway integrations (Stripe Elements, PayPal Hosted Fields, Razorpay) so that sensitive cardholder data never touches your server directly, ensuring full PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance and end-to-end SSL encryption.

Can you migrate our store from an older system without losing SEO rankings or customer data?

Yes. We execute structured zero-downtime database migrations for customers, past order histories, and product taxonomies while preserving URL structures with comprehensive 301 redirect mappings to maintain existing Google organic rankings.
